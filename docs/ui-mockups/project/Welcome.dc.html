Welcome To NetworkAutoSwitch

Three quick things to set up.
Keeps you online when your main connection drops, without letting the backup spend your mobile data.
1
Pick your networks
Tell it which Wi-Fi is main and which is your phone. It asks once per new network.
2
Add programs to watch
Big downloaders get blocked on the backup, then moved back when main returns.
3
Set a daily cap
Get warned at 80 % of the data you allow the backup.
Needs administrator rights to change metrics, firewall and QoS rules. Your original settings are saved first and restored on exit.
